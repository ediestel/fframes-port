# Objects of the Golden Spurs window, for fframes-vector/tools/propose_zones.py
rose/lion: the black lion on the yellow disc in the centre of the rose window at the top
rose/tracery: the round rose window at the top around the lion, with its small coloured panes
spandrels: the small triangular panes left and right of the rose window
sky: the pale sky panes at the tops of the two pointed lancet windows
banners/french: the blue banners with golden lilies (fleurs-de-lis)
banners/flanders: the yellow banner with the black lion
battle/french/lances: the long lances of the mounted knights
battle/french/knights: the armoured French knights on horseback, upper left lancet
battle/french/horse: the brown horses of the knights
battle/french/fallen_knight: the knight falling or lying on the ground, left lancet
battle/flemish/militia: the Flemish foot soldiers with goedendags and pikes, right lancet
spurs: the small golden spurs lying in the grass, lower right
water: the blue brooks and water at the bottom of both lancets
land/meadow: the green meadow and ground
land/hill: the hill in the background behind the soldiers
inscription: the text panels at the very bottom (GULDENSPORENSLAG, KORTRIJK 1302)
